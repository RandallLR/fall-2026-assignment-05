About Our Business...

WorldWide Finds is a store that has a little bit of everything! Not everyone can go out and explore the world, so let us bring a piece of it to you! If you want something but don't see it in-store, drop a suggestion in our very own item suggestion box near the front of the store by customer services!

-------------------------------------------------

Not everyone has the privilege to venture out of their state or country and learn more about the world. We here at WorldWide Finds we aim to help alleviate that struggle and bring the world to you! Being able to explore a bit of the world growing up along with loving to help others made me decide to create a Business for others to have a taste of what it's like outside of the states.
